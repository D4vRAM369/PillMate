PillMate is for medication you take <b>when you need it</b>, not on a fixed schedule. Log each dose with one tap, then see your own pattern: when you take it, how much, and how far apart.

It is not an alarm-based pill reminder. Reminders exist, but they are optional.

<b>Features</b>
• One-tap dose logging, with home-screen widget and quick-log notification
• Daily 24-hour clock, weekly view and statistics with calculated insights
• Optional reminders
• Photos of your medication and encrypted voice notes
• Optional lock with fingerprint or face (checked by Android)
• Backup to ZIP, CSV and PDF export, always to a place you choose

<b>Privacy</b>
• No internet permission: the app cannot connect to any server
• No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers
• All data stays on your device
• Free software, GPL-3.0-or-later

PillMate records what you write down; it gives no medical advice and is not a medical device.
